Admin Vehicle Check

Apartment registration

This check helps the administrator verify whether a vehicle belongs to a registered apartment resident.

The system searches the registered vehicle database using the vehicle registration number.

Verification Process

1
Enter Vehicle Number

Admin enters the vehicle registration number.

2
Search Vehicle Records

Firebase searches the registered vehicle database.

3
Identify Resident

The registered vehicle owner information is retrieved.

4
Display Vehicle Details

Vehicle and resident information is shown to the administrator.

5
Admin Decision

Admin can identify the vehicle as registered or unregistered.

What this check does

  • Checks whether the vehicle is registered.
  • Shows the registered resident.
  • Shows Door Number.
  • Shows vehicle type and details.
  • Helps admins identify unknown vehicles.
